# PostCard: App Overview & User Benefits Guide

**Transforming Physical Press, Book Excerpts, and Web Articles into Curated Social Posters**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**PostCard** is an AI-powered visual curation studio designed to turn daily reading habits into publish-ready social capital. Instead of letting highlighted passages, newspaper clippings, or saved web links sit forgotten in camera rolls or bookmarks, PostCard transforms them into **museum-grade 4:5 portrait social feed graphics** paired with sharp, high-signal editorial commentary in under 15 seconds.

PostCard bridges three key stages of modern reading:
1. **What You Read**: Physical broadsheets, book passages, or digital articles.
2. **What You Think (Curator Angle)**: Your authentic, unhedged personal stance.
3. **How It Resonates (Target Audience)**: Re-framed into the vocabulary and stakes of your community.

---

## 2. Core Capabilities & Input Modes

### A. Physical Press to Digital Art (Paper Cut OCR)
* **How it works**: Users snap a photo of a morning newspaper, magazine column, or trade journal.
* **Extraction**: Optical Character Recognition (OCR) reads printed headlines, publication names, bylines, dates, pull quotes, key metrics, and column body text.
* **Transformation**: Converts raw physical print into modern digital poster artwork with verified publication tags.

### B. Book Excerpts & Literary Reflections
* **How it works**: Users photograph a passage or underline from a physical book and capture a photo of the book cover.
* **Literary Anchoring**: Anchors the post to the exact book title, author, chapter, section, or page number (e.g. *Meditations • Marcus Aurelius, Book IV, §3*).
* **Interactive Cover Art**: Tapping the book icon in the feed opens the high-resolution book cover viewer dialog.

### C. Digital Web Article Distiller
* **How it works**: Users paste any digital article URL.
* **Smart Web Scraper**: Bypasses clutter, extracts headlines, publication metadata, and body text.
* **Resilience**: Even if anti-bot shields trigger (HTTP 403), the engine infers the story slug and synthesizes the core takeaways seamlessly.

---

## 3. The Two-Engine Editorial Philosophy

Most AI summarizers fail because they produce bland, hedged press summaries that say *"on the one hand, but on the other hand"*. PostCard replaces this with a two-part editorial engine:

### 1. The Curator Angle (Ownership & Conviction)
* The user provides their personal opinion, critique, warning, or enthusiasm.
* PostCard acts as a ghostwriter that **takes full personal ownership** of this stance in the headline, opening hook, and key arguments.

### 2. The Target Audience Lens (Resonance & Mental Models)
The same Curator Angle is translated to land with specific groups:
* **Startup Founders**: Focuses on burn rate, API commoditization, runway, and moats.
* **Busy Executives**: Focuses on ROI, capital allocation, efficiency, and vendor risks.
* **Tech Enthusiasts / Engineers**: Focuses on architecture, latency, infrastructure, and technical debt.
* **Gen-Z / Social**: High-energy, punchy, unfiltered, and culturally relatable.
* **Custom Audiences**: Define any niche (*e.g., Deep Learning Engineers, Venture Capitalists, Healthcare Workers, Stoic Readers*).

## 4. Visual Poster Design Engine

PostCard generates museum-grade graphics formatted for Instagram (4:5 portrait format), LinkedIn feeds, and messaging:

* **Visual Art & Infographic Ratio Slider (20% – 90%)**:
  * **>= 70% Art Focus**: Hero picture artwork and infographic data visualizations dominate the poster with punchy typography overlays.
  * **45% – 65% Balanced**: 50/50 split between visual art/infographics and curated editorial takeaways.
  * **<= 40% Editorial**: Text-heavy broadsheet analysis with an artistic masthead banner and metric badges.

* **4 Aesthetic Poster Styles**:
  1. **Editorial Broadsheet**: Classic serif typography, prestige newsstand aesthetic, paper textures.
  2. **Modern Cyber**: Dark mode neon, futuristic blueprint grids, data badges, and terminal aesthetics.
  3. **Bold Social**: High-contrast, vibrant, street-poster graphic style.
  4. **Minimalist Swiss**: Clean Scandinavian whitespace, asymmetric layout, and bold grotesque typography.
